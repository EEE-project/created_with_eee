## literal

<!-- **literal** · a literal English rendering written for this course from the Greek text and its word-by-word gloss, line for line with the Greek -->

### Ιθάκη, στ. 1–3

When you set out on the journey for Ithaca,
wish that the road be long,
full of adventures, full of knowledge.

### Ιθάκη, στ. 1–3

When you start on the way to Ithaca,
wish that the way be long,
full of adventure, full of knowledge.
