### Ιθάκη, στ. 4–12

The Laestrygonians and the Cyclopes,
the angry Poseidon, do not fear,
such things on your road you will never find,
if your thought remains elevated, if exquisite
emotion touches your spirit and your body.
The Laestrygonians and the Cyclopes,
the wild Poseidon you will not meet,
if you do not carry them inside your soul,
if your soul does not set them up before you.

## Valassopoulo

<!-- **G. Valassopoulo, 1924** · the first published English translation, in *The Criterion* (July 1924) · public domain in the US · text via [ornaverum.org](https://ornaverum.org/misc/epilogue/06.html) -->

### Ιθάκη, στ. 4–12

The Laestrygones and the Cyclopes
and angry Poseidon, do not fear:
such, on your way, you shall never meet
if your thoughts are lofty, if a noble
emotion touch your mind, your body.
The Laestrygones and the Cyclopes
and angry Poseidon you shall not meet
if you carry them not in your soul,
if your soul sets them not up before you.
