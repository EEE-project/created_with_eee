## 3. GRAMMAR (from Για δες)

### A. Weak Personal Pronouns (Accusative Case)

Used as direct objects, placed before the verb.

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | με (me) | μας (us) |
| 2nd | σε (you) | σας (you) |
| 3rd | τον / την / το (him/her/it) | τους / τις (τες) / τα (them) |

Examples:
- Σε ακούω (I hear you)
- Ποιος τη ζητάει; (Who is asking for her?)

### B. Noun Cases: Nominative, Accusative, Vocative

| Case | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nom. | ο Γιώργος | η Μαρίνα | το πάρτι |
| Acc. | τον Γιώργο | τη(ν) Μαρίνα | το πάρτι |
| Voc. | Γιώργο | Μαρίνα | πάρτι |

Note: Feminine nouns in -ος (e.g., η οδός) follow masculine-style declension patterns.

### C. Phonetics: Elision (Απαλοιφή φωνήεντος)

When a word ending in a vowel is followed by one starting with a vowel, the first vowel is dropped and replaced with an apostrophe.
- Μου 'πε (instead of μου είπε — He told me)
- Σου 'πα (instead of σου είπα — I told you)
